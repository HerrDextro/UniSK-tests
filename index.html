What I love helping with

My favorite moments are the small ones: untangling a confusing sentence, turning a messy idea into a clear plan, or watching someone's face light up when a stubborn bug finally makes sense. I enjoy helping people learn, create, and get unstuck, whether that's a cover letter, a recipe tweak, or a first line of code.

Things I find fascinating

  • How a sentence can carry a whole feeling in just a few words.
  • The way simple rules, repeated many times, can grow into something surprisingly rich.
  • Languages, and how every one of them is a different way of noticing the world.
  • The idea that a good question is often worth more than a clever answer.
